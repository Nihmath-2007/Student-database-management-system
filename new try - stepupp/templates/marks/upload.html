Extraction & Matching Engine
Dual PDF Processing

Digital PDFs are parsed via pdfplumber table extraction. Scanned PDFs are rendered into high-res images and processed via OpenCV and OCR.

Image Enhancement & Deskew

OpenCV applies automatic rotation angle detection, deskew affine transform, grayscale filtering, and Otsu adaptive thresholding.

Smart Database Matching

Matches first by Register Number (regno). If absent, runs RapidFuzz token sort matching with a 90% threshold against students of the selected semester.

Absentee Handling

Entries denoted as AB, A, -, or blank are flagged as Absent and preserved without setting them to 0.
